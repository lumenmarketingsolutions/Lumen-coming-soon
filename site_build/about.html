About

Because we were told it was impossible.Because we were told it was impossible.

So we built it anyway, from pure creativity and passion, one layer at a time.

The name

Latin for light.

The light always wins in the dark. A source of truth shines on anything that is not. That is the idea our whole company is built on.

The story

Light comes from layers.

A star makes light by layering one material over another until it starts to glow on its own. That is how Lumen was built.

Years of studying how people think and decide. Audience psychology, ad platforms, landing pages, copy, follow up, automation. Layer after layer, until it produced its own light.

Now we use it to clear the way for the businesses we work with. Every friction point, every broken funnel, every dollar going out and not coming back. We find it and we fix it.

We want people to come to us because what we offer is valuable, and because we know we can make a real difference in their business.

The mission

To be the source of truth.

We are not trying to be the loudest agency or the biggest one. We want to be the most honest one. The place business owners come when they are tired of hearing what they want to hear, and ready for what they need to hear.

When we say yes to someone, we do not step backwards. Your brand is treated like ours. Your growth is our growth.
