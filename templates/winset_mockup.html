How it works

Three steps. You keep the keys.

Most people expect to hand money to someone. That is exactly what does not happen here.

1

You open your own broker account

In your name, at a regulated broker, with your own login. You fund it, you can see it any time, and you can withdraw any time.

2

Rudin connects and trades by its rules

The algorithm is linked to your account with trade only permission. It cannot withdraw. It follows a fixed rule set on EUR/USD with position size and loss limits defined in advance.

3

Winset earns only when you profit

There is no monthly fee and no charge on losses. At the end of each period, Winset takes an agreed share of the profit, and only the profit. If there is none, you owe nothing.

Questions

The things people ask on the call

Does my money go to Winset?

No. Your capital is deposited by you into a broker account that is in your name. Winset is connected with trade only permission, which means it can open and close trades but cannot withdraw or transfer anything. You keep full access.

Is there risk?

Yes. Trading involves risk of loss and past results do not predict future results. Rudin runs with a defined maximum risk per trade and does not use martingale or grid methods, which limits how bad a losing run can get, but it does not remove risk. Only use capital you can afford to put at risk.

What does it cost?

A share of profit, agreed in writing before you start. There is no subscription and no charge in a period with no profit. The exact percentage depends on the account size and is explained on the call.

What is Rudin, exactly?

Winset's flagship algorithm. It trades EUR/USD only, has run live since September 2020, and was built by mathematicians on about twenty years of market data using statistical methods rather than technical or fundamental analysis. Every rule and risk limit is fixed in advance.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes. Pause the connection or withdraw from your broker at any time. There is no notice period and no exit fee.
